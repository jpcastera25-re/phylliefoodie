About Phyllie

A small kitchen project by Phyllie

Phyllie started Phyllie’s Foodie’s with a simple idea: bake the kind of food people are excited to share. What began with homemade breads for friends, coworkers, and family is now growing into a small kitchen project filled with fresh loaves, thoughtful flavors, and a whole lot of care.

Helpful details

Cottage Food & Allergen Notice

Phyllie’s Foodie’s operates as a Florida cottage food operation. Products are made in a home kitchen and are packaged with required cottage food labeling.

“Made in a cottage food operation that is not subject to Florida’s food safety regulations.”

Products may contain or be prepared around common allergens, including wheat, milk, eggs, tree nuts, peanuts, soy, sesame, and other ingredients. Please ask about ingredients before ordering if you have a food allergy or dietary concern.

Ingredient lists, net weight or volume, and allergen details should be confirmed on the packaged product label for each item.
