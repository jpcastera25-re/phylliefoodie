About Phyllie

A small kitchen project by Phyllis

Phyllis started Phyllie’s Foodie’s with a simple idea: bake the kind of food people are excited to share. What began with homemade breads for friends, coworkers, and family is now growing into a small kitchen project filled with fresh loaves, thoughtful flavors, and a whole lot of care.
